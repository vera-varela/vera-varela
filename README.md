# 👩‍💻 Vera Varela

**`Data Scientist`**

I have a Master’s degree in Data Science and Analytics and more than 9 years of experience in the data field, across Data Science, Data Analysis, Data Engineering, and Business Intelligence. In Data Science, I have developed projects where I was the sole person responsible, involving Machine Learning, Segmentation and Profiling, and Natural Language Processing (NLP), covering all phases of the process (from data extraction and exploratory data analysis (EDA) to model development, evaluation, and interpretation of results). I also have professional experience working on Machine Learning, Next Best Action, and Conversational AI projects as part of a team.

I am available for freelance Data Science projects involving:
- Machine Learning
- Mathematical Optimization
- Deep Learning
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Large Language Models (LLMs)

If you have a Data Science project you would like to discuss, feel free to contact me on LinkedIn or send me an email.
